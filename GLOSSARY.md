# Contac

Contac is a contact and interview log for university teams that coordinate outreach to industry contacts within a class.

## Language

**Call guide**:
A team-owned document of talking points that a member reads during a live conversation. A team can have several, each named for the kind of contact or conversation it suits. UI label: `Samtaleguide`.
_Avoid_: Script, call script, playbook, template

**Live conversation**:
A `Telefonsamtale` or `Nytt møte` activity while its composer is open. Email is not a live conversation.
_Avoid_: Active call, session
